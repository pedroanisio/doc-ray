# Doc-Ray 1.48.0

Version 1.48.0 adds relationship discovery over the entity graph: ask a lens
"who are the significant figures in this body of work, and how do they relate?"
and get a precomputed, cited answer.

## Who is affected

- **Readers and researchers** — two new questions become answerable per lens:
  the most significant entities (by mention and document volume, with an
  observed type profile and a first-seen citation), and which entities relate
  to a given one, explored a bounded number of hops out.
- **Integrators (API / MCP)** — new reads: `lensEntities` (now with a minimum-
  mentions filter, type profile, and staleness stamp), `entityRelations`, and
  `relatedEntities`. The MCP server mirrors all three.

## What to know before relying on it

- Relationship strength is a **co-occurrence measure** (how often two entities
  share a sentence, normalized so a ubiquitous entity is not "related" to
  everything). It is **not a claim of a real-world relationship** — treat it
  as a lead to verify against the cited sentences.
- Results are **precomputed** and carry a `refreshedAt` stamp; after heavy
  ingestion they can lag until the refresh job runs.
- Hop exploration is bounded; a request beyond the cap is rejected rather
  than silently clamped.

## Action required

None. The rollups build automatically after entity refreshes; administrators
can trigger a full rebuild via the refresh job if a lens looks stale.
