# Doc-Ray 1.45.0

Version 1.45.0 gives every document a **genre axis** — fiction, non-fiction,
mixed, or unknown — as the foundation for fiction-aware entity identity.

## Who is affected

- **Curators** — a deterministic classifier (no language model) proposes a
  genre from observable signals such as dialogue density, narration style,
  and chapter structure. The guess stays **pending** until a human confirms
  it; the system never asserts a machine guess as fact.
- **Integrators (API / MCP)** — new `documentClassification` read and guarded
  `classifyDocument` / `confirmDocumentClassification` writes, mirrored over
  MCP.

## What to know

- In this release the genre **defines policy only** — a per-entity-type rule
  for whether an entity should resolve locally (inside a fiction's own scope)
  or globally (shared identity), with a conservative global default for
  real-world types like dates and laws even inside fiction. Resolution
  behavior itself changes in 1.46.0, which consumes this policy.

## Action required

None. Existing documents are unclassified until the job runs or a curator
classifies them; nothing resolves differently yet.
