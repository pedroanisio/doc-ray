Guide

doc-ray guide

doc-ray turns source documents into an auditable corpus: ingest files, annotate text into linguistic layers, search the results, and curate domain entities before they enter the shared canonical registry.

Reading Pipeline status Domain seeding Backups

Reading

The corpus page lists ingested documents. Open a document to read sentence pages with part-of-speech annotation overlaid; click any annotated word to inspect its lexeme and keyword-in-context examples.

Pipeline status

Uploads enqueue background jobs. The status page refreshes while work is queued or running, then links to the document and its curation screen.

  1. Ingest stores the source and extracted text.
  2. Annotate builds sentences, lexemes, and entity mentions.
  3. Canonicalize links known entities when the registry can resolve them.

Domain seeding

Seeding is the human audit loop for adding reliable entities to the canonical registry. Mine candidates, audit every identity, approve only exact matches, then seed the approved rows.

Backups

Backups capture the corpus database and stored blobs as one archive. Restore is destructive: it rewinds the corpus to that archive, so confirm the target snapshot before starting a restore job.
