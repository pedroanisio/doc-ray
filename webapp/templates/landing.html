Document intelligence platform

Turn documents into an auditable research corpus.

Point doc·ray at your PDFs, EPUBs, and Office files. It extracts the text, decomposes it to sentences, tags every word for part of speech, canonicalizes the entities, and serves the whole corpus through a fast reader and a single GraphQL API — with every result traceable back to the source.

How it works

01 · Extract

Ingest & extract

Text and metadata from PDF, EPUB, TXT, MD, and DOCX. Original bytes are stored content-addressed in S3 / MinIO.

02 · Annotate

Annotate

A 20-layer linguistic annotation graph over every sentence — lemmas, parts of speech, and dependency structure.

03 · Canonicalize

Canonicalize entities

Entity mentions are resolved to canonical, deduplicated identities you can curate.

04 · Enrich

Enrich

Semantic embeddings connect related passages so retrieval reaches beyond exact wording.

05 · Query

Query

A typed GraphQL API plus full-text and keyword-in-context search over the whole corpus.

What you get

Multi-format ingestion

PDF, EPUB, TXT, MD, and DOCX in; a normalized, full-text corpus out.

20-layer annotation graph

Every sentence carries a rich linguistic annotation layer you can read alongside the source.

Entity canonicalization

Mentions collapse to canonical identities, with a review path for curation.

Semantic enrichment

Embeddings connect related passages so retrieval and comparison work across the whole corpus.

GraphQL API

A typed Strawberry GraphQL surface over the corpus, with role-scoped access.

Full-text & KWIC search

Search the corpus and read matches in keyword-in-context, sentence by sentence.
